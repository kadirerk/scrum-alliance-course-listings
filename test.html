Program Modules

Across six self‑paced modules you’ll build a product from idea to investor‑ready pitch:

  • Module 1: Product & Customer Discovery
  • Module 2: Defining, Testing & Measuring Hypotheses
  • Module 3: Defining Vision & Strategy
  • Module 4: Leadership Skills for Product Owners
  • Module 5: Advanced Roadmapping & Refinement
  • Module 6: Product Ownership with Multiple Teams

Successful completion of all 6 modules plus two live Product Lab sessions will earn your A‑CSPO accreditation.

You’ll schedule two live Product Lab mentoring sessions: one after you complete Module 3: Defining Vision & Strategy, and a final five‑minute Shark Tank–style pitch after Module 6: Product Ownership with Multiple Teams. Work through the modules at your own pace; the whole program remains open for six months.
